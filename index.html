A poem for my princess who became a queen

The Queen

There once was a princess, one that was precious and rare No one dared to get close, as they feared her stare A boy got close without a clue at all The princess smiled and let down her wall.
He saw she was soft as a delicate flower Ambitious and bright, growing stronger each hour So beautiful, kind, working hard through the day She chased after dreams in her own brilliant way.
Nobody knows just how or when But the two of them grew closer then. And the bright little princess became a queen With the kindest soul he has ever seen.
The boy tried to be a poet, he was terrible and he knew it. The queen knows too, but she lets him show it.
As life goes on, their bond grows strong, and whatever may come, they will still belong, like the sun and the moon sharing one sky, neither one will ever say goodbye.

Happy Birthday, Imane
